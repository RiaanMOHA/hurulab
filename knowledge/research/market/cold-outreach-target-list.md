# hurulab cold-outreach target list: Taiwanese businesses ranked by digital weakness

## TL;DR
- The strongest low-hanging fruit are heritage food, tea, and traditional retail businesses that run on phone or Facebook only despite very high demand, plus one export manufacturer whose website has been frozen since roughly 2014. Top five: 龍都酒樓 (Long Du Restaurant), 王有記名茶 (Wang's Tea, wangstea.com.tw), 林華泰茶行 (Lin Hua Tai Tea), 皇盈 ALLPRO (tin-snip exporter), and 銀翼餐廳 (Yin Yih Restaurant).
- Rank is by weakness, not size. The defining signals found: no website at all, a broken decade-old website, no e-commerce despite selling premium physical goods, phone-only booking with public frustration, and no English despite obvious international customers.
- Two subsidy doors are live and time-sensitive: the "≤30 employees" digital transformation subsidy (NT$10,000 per employee, max NT$100,000 per business, NT$2 billion budgeted for 2025) and, for manufacturers with a named successor, the SME manufacturing succession digital transformation grant (planning case up to NT$5,000,000, practice case up to NT$15,000,000, government share capped at 50%). These are strong openers for the family-owned, mid-succession targets.

## Key findings
- The single most common weakness across premium Taiwanese food and drink is phone-only ordering and booking at businesses with thousands of reviews. This is a direct, quantifiable revenue leak (missed calls, no-shows, no capture of tourist or overseas demand).
- Heritage tea houses in Dadaocheng are a target-rich cluster: several of the oldest names either have no e-commerce or run a broken, machine-garbled legacy website.
- Silver and gold jewellery shops (銀樓) are almost uniformly websiteless, but most are small; include only where the weakness is explicit and the brand has premium positioning.
- The best B2B manufacturing target found is 皇盈 (ALLPRO), a global tin-snip exporter with a second-generation VP and a website whose news feed stops in 2000 and whose footer reads copyright 2014.
- Subsidy handling is a genuine door-opener: most targets qualify for at least the ≤30-employee track, and the manufacturers with a named successor qualify for the succession-focused grant.

## Details: ranked targets

### 1. 龍都酒樓 (Long Du Restaurant), Taipei
- The company: about 40-year-old Cantonese roast-duck restaurant, Zhongshan and Neihu branches. Per Long Du's official site, the founder, a builder working on the 六條華廈 project in 1979, "在1983年創立了龍都酒樓，開啟了粵菜餐館的經營之路." Family-run old-guard establishment. Famous after NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang (黃仁勳) held a 25-table banquet at the Neihu branch ("AI教父黃仁勳宴請法人25桌就在這兒【龍都酒樓內湖店】").
- Website: has a basic official site and Facebook page; no Instagram, no email, no delivery, no online booking.
- Diagnosed weakness: phone-only reservation despite over 8,500 Google reviews (icecreamcat.tw: "在網路上龍都酒樓擁有超過8500多條評論"; blake.com.tw: "在網路上的評價超過8000則以上"). Multiple bloggers report it is nearly impossible to book, lines jam, and there is no online option. One blogger's data card explicitly lists "Email: 無 / Instagram: 無 / Uber Eats: 無 / foodpanda: 無" (jayhellola.com). A public notice reads that from 16 April, due to staff shortage, phone and platform pre-ordering of dishes and dim sum was suspended (travelss.net). This is a business turning away demand it cannot physically answer by phone.
- Cost of the weakness: with dinner slots fully booked daily and a named-celebrity demand spike, every unanswered call is a lost NT$10,000-plus table. No waitlist capture, no deposit system to fight no-shows, no overseas or tourist pre-order.
- Contact route: no verified individual name found; treat ownership as unknown. Best channels: Facebook page message, then in-person at the Zhongshan branch during off-peak (approximately 2pm to 5pm), then the contact number on the official site. A 104人力銀行 application is not confirmed viable (no posting verified).
- The hook: "You are famous for being impossible to book, and your own notice says you had to suspend phone pre-orders because of staffing. A booking-plus-deposit system would recover the tables you are turning away, without adding staff."
- Draft message (English): "Hello Long Du team. You are one of the most in-demand roast-duck restaurants in Taipei, yet booking is phone-only and your notice mentioned suspending pre-orders due to staffing. We build working reservation and deposit systems fast, and we would hand the tool over to your team. Could we show you a live demo built on your actual menu in a 20-minute meeting?"
- Draft message (Traditional Chinese): "龍都酒樓團隊您好。貴店是台北最熱門的烤鴨餐廳之一，但目前只能電話訂位，且公告曾因人力暫停預訂。我們能快速做出可用的線上訂位與訂金系統，並把工具與操作交給您的團隊。可否給我們20分鐘，看一個用貴店真實菜單做的實機示範？"
- Kickoff conversion play: pre-build a clickable booking flow seeded with their real roast-duck pre-order rule (one duck per table) and a deposit step to kill no-shows. First meeting proves that a booking system reduces phone load and captures the demand they currently lose.

### 4. 皇盈企業 / ALLPROFESSIONAL MFG. (ALLPRO), Taichung

Global tin-snip market share is 35%, per VP 黃崇斌: "現在全球市面上多達35%的鐵皮剪，都是由皇盈企業所製造，等於每三支鐵皮剪裡面就有一支是由皇盈企業的工廠而來" (domestic share also cited as over 70%, olily.com). There is no modern product configurator, no bilingual mobile catalog, and the firm appears mainly through third-party directories (CENS, Taiwan Hand Tools Association).
