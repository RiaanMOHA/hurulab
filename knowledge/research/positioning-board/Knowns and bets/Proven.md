# Proven

These are the things we actually know.

- Our own experience hiring consultancies was poor.
- Jadegia came to us because the alternatives were over-sized with long delivery tails.
- Inkslap came to us because their previous partners were led by weak process that produced lacklustre delivery.
- Jadegia's business goal is stated as growing 20% of their revenue from their online storefront.
- Our rapid-prototype approach wowed both Jadegia and Inkslap: a workable rebuild in days, not months.
- With Jadegia, Dada and Jiuyang, reaching an international audience is a real, stated client need: Jadegia's overseas storefront, Dada's Chinese-only site opened up to global investors, and Jiuyang's push into Japan. Internationalisation is a lever, not the lead; the measurable result is still to be proven.
- Referrals already compound: one existing relationship brought two paid projects at once (Dada and Jiuyang), the exact flywheel our growth engine depends on.
