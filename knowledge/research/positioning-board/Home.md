# hurulab, positioning and decisions

Vault built from the Miro board *hurulab: Foundation decisions*.
Source: https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVH7rsHaY=/

> **What this is, and how to read it.** A transcription of the owner's own Miro board into one
> page per sticky. It carries **no date**: the board was undated and none of these files
> records when it was written. The board sits between the July interviews and the 4 August
> meeting on internal evidence alone, so treat every statement here as undated.
>
> **It is the second-strongest source in `knowledge/`**, after the interviews, because it is
> the owner's own words rather than anyone's synthesis. `Knowns and bets/` is the most useful
> part: the owner's own split between what is established and what is not.
>
> The `[[double bracket]]` links are Obsidian syntax and do not resolve on GitHub. Navigate
> by folder instead. Everything the board contained is here; nothing was added.
>
> Six decisions in `Questions/` were never closed. See `../../contradictions.md`.
