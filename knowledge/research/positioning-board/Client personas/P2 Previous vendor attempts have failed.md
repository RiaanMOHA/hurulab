# Previous attempts to work with vendors have failed

**How they are:** Already spent time and/or costs with other vendors but were badly let down. Still have the ambitions if they find the right partner.

**What's holding them back:** Distrust. Won't commit until they have proof that something real, working, and aligned with their problem can be delivered.

**A big open question:** Does a prototype win them over, or is it 'smoke and mirrors' that just gets admired?

**A pitch that needs to be tested:** Speed can backfire. A quick prototype could look like another sales trick or an over-promise.

**How sure are we?** **Medium / Strong** — adding a prototype into the pitch has so far worked well for Inklap and Jadegia.
