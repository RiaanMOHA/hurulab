# Sells online through intermediary platforms

**How they are:** Established business that does sell online, but through partner platforms (Shopee, Uber Eats, Food Panda, etc.).

**What's holding them back:** "I'm already online and it works." They don't feel the real cost: the 20–35% commission, and the marketplace owning the customer relationship, the brand and the data.

**A big open question:** Will an owner who's comfortable on a marketplace invest in a channel they own, when the marketplace already brings the orders?

**A pitch that needs to be tested:** No test run. The pitch is: you're online, but you're renting when you could own. No longer have someone else taking a cut, but would lose their customer relationships. Likely a warmer cold target than the offline owner, because the pain is nameable.

**How sure are we?** **Weak** — no evidence these owners exist, but it might be a safe bet there is a market.
