# Doesn't sell online or no website presence

**How they are:** Established business, face-to-face, word of mouth or possibly socials. Weak / no website, no website sales.

**What's holding them back:** Customers prefer traditional brick & mortar shopping or through LINE. Nothing feels broken.

**A big open question:** Will an owner who feels no pain pay for a problem they cannot see?

**A pitch that needs to be tested:** A prototype probably won't cut it — sales data as evidence to convince them. Either from clients we have helped, public data from their competitors, or broader sector data.

**How sure are we?** **Medium / Strong** — the hardware store prospect may validate this.
