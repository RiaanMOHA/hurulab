# Already know they have a problem (Jadegia)

**How they are:** Solid business, with an under-performing website. Ambitious, cares about quality, and their site is holding them back.

**What's holding them back:** Sold on the fact that they must do something, but their current site makes little revenue — it's still a gamble.

**A big open question:** Will a website actually increase sales, or does the current business model work well enough no matter how good a website there is?

**A pitch that needs to be tested:** Sort out which products work better for eCommerce vs in-person and which to push online first. Is the goal to grow online revenue or a funnel towards the traditional business?

**How sure are we?** **Strong** — Jadegia fits this category but results are yet to be proven.
