## Full matrix

| Owner | How they are | What's holding them back | A big open question | A pitch to test | How sure are we? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[P1 Already know they have a problem\|Already know they have a problem (Jadegia)]] | Solid business, with an under-performing website. Ambitious, cares about quality, and their site is holding them back. | Sold on the fact that they must do something, but their current site makes little revenue — it's still a gamble. | Will a website actually increase sales, or does the current business model work well enough no matter how good a website there is? | Sort out which products work better for eCommerce vs in-person and which to push online first. Is the goal to grow online revenue or a funnel towards the traditional business? | Strong — Jadegia fits this category but results are yet to be proven. |
| [[P2 Previous vendor attempts have failed\|Previous attempts to work with vendors have failed]] | Already spent time and/or costs with other vendors but were badly let down. Still have the ambitions if they find the right partner. | Distrust. Won't commit until they have proof that something real, working, and aligned with their problem can be delivered. | Does a prototype win them over, or is it 'smoke and mirrors' that just gets admired? | Speed can backfire. A quick prototype could look like another sales trick or an over-promise. | Medium / Strong — adding a prototype into the pitch has so far worked well for Inklap and Jadegia. |
| [[P3 Doesn't sell online\|Doesn't sell online or no website presence]] | Established business, face-to-face, word of mouth or possibly socials. Weak / no website, no website sales. | Customers prefer traditional brick & mortar shopping or through LINE. Nothing feels broken. | Will an owner who feels no pain pay for a problem they cannot see? | A prototype probably won't cut it — sales data as evidence to convince them. Either from clients we have helped, public data from their competitors, or broader sector data. | Medium / Strong — the hardware store prospect may validate this. |
| [[P4 Start-up founder\|A start-up founder, no proven business and technical capabilities]] | An ambitious founder with a clear idea but no team to build it. Their worry is feeling out of their depth, costs and other unknowns. | Not tested. | Not tested. | This is a theoretical client for now, but a completely separate prospect as there is no proven business. | Weak — take this seriously and size the market, or rule it out? |
| [[P5 Sells online via intermediary platforms\|Sells online through intermediary platforms]] | Established business that does sell online, but through partner platforms (Shopee, Uber Eats, Food Panda, etc.). | "I'm already online and it works." They don't feel the real cost: the 20–35% commission, and the marketplace owning the customer relationship, the brand and the data. | Will an owner who's comfortable on a marketplace invest in a channel they own, when the marketplace already brings the orders? | No test run. The pitch is: you're online, but you're renting when you could own. No longer have someone else taking a cut, but would lose their customer relationships. Likely a warmer cold target than the offline owner, because the pain is nameable. | Weak — no evidence these owners exist, but it might be a safe bet there is a market. |
