# Who we go after

Established Asian companies, ambitious and quality-minded, not chasing cheap, that want a partner to hit a named goal. Two shapes:

1. A healthy offline business but an online sales or marketing channel that badly underperforms or is yet to exist.
2. A business with a particular challenge that demands deep engineering, frustrated by traditional process lag.
