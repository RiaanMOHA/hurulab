# Value proposition

hurulab is a design and engineering consultancy for established Asian companies that want more from their software. We answer to the goal, not a feature list, then build exactly what gets you there, whether that's winning new customers or running the operation better. Design and engineering sit in one team. We prove the solution fits the problem with usable prototypes, not a backlog of requests, before we commit to it.
