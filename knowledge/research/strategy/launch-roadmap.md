## The six workstreams

Every phase advances six parallel workstreams: brand (identity, voice, site), proof (instrumented results, case stories), presence (the minimum visible stack referrers check), pipeline (demos, talks, referrals), method (the productized way of working), and ring 2 (expansion prep). The rule from document 3 stands: if a benchmark misses by more than a quarter, the correction is narrower targets and more teaser demos, not broader marketing.

## Phase 0: foundations (July to August 2026)

The brand and the proof engine get built before any outbound motion starts.

- Brand: finalize brand strategy and verbal identity (document 7), Chinese brand name decision, visual identity designed by Riaan, bilingual site shipped as the first demonstration of the method (story-driven, tappable, fast, the site itself is case zero). Per document 5: six-word headline, named engagement models, founder as supervisor of record on the about page.
- Proof: capture Jadegia launch numbers and one recorded owner sentence at launch. Get the GKTK reuse quote on record. Adopt the standing contract clause: two numbers at launch plus 90 days, quote may be requested.
- Presence: stand up the minimum stack referrers check: Facebook page, Instagram or Behance portfolio, LINE official account, Chinese-language site presence. Skip Dribbble and Clutch.
- Method: write the public method page (the audit trail, the locked source of truth, the five caught audits) as the governance proof. Publish the plain-language contract in Chinese (the MDS move, novel in this market).
- Exit criteria: site live, Jadegia numbers captured, presence stack live.

## Phase 1: proof and platform (September to October 2026)

- Proof: publish the first two case stories in the Pixar structure with impact numbers first (the Ustwo order) and one outcome sentence per client for the work index (the Work & Co format). Bilingual.
- Pipeline: build the qualified target list, 50 heritage businesses matching the symptom, desk-audited. Prepare the first teaser demo candidates. Frame all teaser demos as invitation-only (never free, per document 4's scam-reflex finding).
- Presence: first method article in Chinese, judgment-versus-tools framing, not pricing keywords. Golden Pin submission prepared for the next cycle (the matchmaking matters as much as the badge).
- Ring 2 seed: none yet; Taiwan only.
- Exit criteria: two published cases with numbers, target list of 50, Golden Pin submission ready.

## Phase 2: the demo engine (November 2026 to February 2027)

- Pipeline: two teaser demos a month to the best targets; goal, one new signed client a quarter minimum. First talk at 二代大學, TDEA, or N世代學苑 with a live demo as the centerpiece ("how a 94-year-old jade house chose its new website by using it").
- Method: stand up the corrected subsidy-handling service: 2026 point programs (up to NT$20,000), the under-30-employee training reimbursement (up to NT$100,000, funds the handover training already in scope), the succession digital transformation program for manufacturing cases, SIIR for innovative builds. TCloud is dead; never cite it.
- Brand: one method article a month; each ends with a live demo link.
- Benchmark: at least 2 external (non-MoreHarvest) clients signed by end of February 2027.
- Exit criteria: demo engine running at cadence, first network talk delivered, two external clients.

## Phase 3: compound (March to June 2027)

- Pipeline: ask each satisfied client for exactly two introductions; the referral chain becomes deliberate. Second and third network talks.
- Proof: instrumented results now exist from at least two projects; refresh all story documents with real step-7 endings. At least one published case with real outcome numbers and a client quote by end of April 2027.
- Brand: pricing review against the AI-native wave (document 4's window is 2 to 4 years and FraserTec suggests the short end; review every six months).
- Watch list: Tenten moving down-market, FraserTec crossing to Taiwan, global studios adding Taiwan pages. If any of the three happens, accelerate proof publishing and consider claiming the succession lane louder.
- Exit criteria: half of new pipeline arriving by referral (the trust-engine signal from document 3).

## Phase 4: ring 2 toes in the water (July to October 2027)

- Ring 2: take the strongest case story to Hong Kong and Singapore through existing networks, one exploratory trip per market, referral-fed only. Note from document 4: FraserTec already sells two-week AI builds in Hong Kong, so the Hong Kong entry leads with governance and design, not speed.
- Japan: assess formally: subsidy vendor registration path (the renamed digital and AI adoption subsidy, up to 4.5 million yen, coverage 50 to 80 percent), one Kumamoto-adjacent pilot beyond MoreHarvest, local partner for registration.
- Year review: pipeline by source, decide year 2 weighting between deepening Taiwan and opening ring 2.

## The five decisions already made (do not reopen without new evidence)

1. Premium pricing holds; concede scope, never price (document 3, confirmed by document 4's tier data).
2. The teaser demo is invitation-only, on the prospect's real brand, two days maximum, mobile-first, one moment of surprise.
3. Published outcome numbers are the second moat; every case ends with two numbers and one quote.
4. The succession lane (接班) is the primary ring 1 wedge.
5. Channels: referral engine plus successor networks plus Golden Pin plus LINE official account. No Dribbble, no marketplaces, Clutch only if foreign inbound matters later.
