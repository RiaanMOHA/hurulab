# hurulab knowledge

Everything behind hurulab's brand: how the research was done, what it found, and what was
built from it. Assembled 4 September 2026 from the working corpus.

**One rule governs this folder.** Every claim about hurulab traces to a source, and the
sources sit here in strength order. Where two documents disagree, the disagreement is written
down rather than smoothed over. See `contradictions.md`.

## The sources, in strength order

**1. The interviews.** `research/interviews/all-five-interviews.md`. Five people, thirty
questions, in their own words. Riaan, Cal and Henry on 8 July 2026, BY on 10 July, Pedro on
15 July. This is the strongest source here. The individual raw responses are in
`research/interviews/raw/`, and the questionnaire they answered is `the-30-questions.md`.

Ed, the CTO, was never interviewed. Several strategy documents describe their conclusions as
provisional pending his input. He has not since been asked.

**2. The positioning board.** `research/positioning-board/`. The company's own foundation
decisions, transcribed from a Miro board. Its most useful pages are
`Knowns and bets/Proven.md` and `Knowns and bets/Still a bet.md`, which are the owner's own
split between what is established and what is not, and `Questions/`, which holds six
positioning decisions that were never closed.

**3. The projects.** `projects/`. Five write-ups, each with an evidence section and an open
gaps section. The gaps sections are the most trustworthy content here, because they are the
authors saying what they could not prove.

**4. The market research.** `research/market/`. Research about Taiwan and the competitive
field. It is not evidence about hurulab, and no claim about hurulab should be taken from it.

## What this folder does not contain

**Client confidential material.** Dada Investment's research, their licence number and
representative, the measured performance findings on their site, and the virtual hardware
store handover all stay out. They belong to clients, not to this repository.

**Invented story passages.** Each project write-up originally carried a "Pixar seven-step"
narrative section about a composite client. They read like history and are not. One was once
mistaken for company history and had to be removed from the brand foundation. They have been
stripped, and each file says where.

## Corrections applied

Three claims were corrected on the way in, because a later document in this same corpus had
already disproved them and the correction was never carried back.

- **The 60 percent failure statistic** is not citable. No survey stands behind it. The
  citable replacement is PwC Taiwan's 2022 figure, 53.5 percent seeing under 5 percent
  revenue increase. Corrected in `research/market/taiwan-market-research.md`.
- **The NT$30,000 TCloud subsidy ended on 15 March 2024.** It was being sold as a live
  door-opener in two documents. Corrected in `taiwan-market-research.md` and
  `research/strategy/go-to-market.md`.
- **The company name is spelled `hurulab`**, one word, lowercase. The corpus used four
  spellings. Normalised throughout, except inside filenames and URLs.
