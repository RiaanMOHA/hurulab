## Problems encountered and how they were solved

1. I proposed instead of building, and the proposal read as a delivery. Riaan asked "what are these files? why did you create these without any feedback from the client?" Nothing existed. The fix: state plainly that nothing exists, then build. With this user, do not write a four-item plan and wait. Build the highest-confidence thing and show it.

2. My audit's finding 3 was wrong. I inferred from the filenames `about_icon-img_r1_c1.jpg`, `_r1_c3`, `_r1_c5`, `_r1_c7` (Photoshop slice-export naming, row 1 column 1) that the about section's text was baked into images, and marked it as unconfirmed for the Chrome research to settle. The research settled it: they are the home page service icons and they carry alt text. I corrected the audit in place and left the correction visible rather than quietly editing it. The real text-in-image problem is the partner diagram on sales.htm. Marking an inference as unconfirmed is what made this recoverable. Keep doing that.

3. The master prompt's section 0 described Dada as a company that "sells Tokyo property". The research proved they are a family firm founded in Tokyo in 2018 by Taiwanese investors that invests its own capital and also brokers. Rewrote section 0 around it. Do not describe a client from a Slack summary when the evidence is one search away.

4. I stalled mid-turn. An AskUserQuestion call errored with an AbortError, and instead of carrying on I emitted "No response requested" while Riaan had sent real instructions mid-turn. He asked "why so slow??". If a question tool errors, do not wait, just act on what you already know.

5. Verbosity, repeatedly. "too much text", "dont understand anything", "what is this?" Three separate times my answer was longer than his patience. The one that worked: a full reset in five lines saying you have one thing that matters today, send the questionnaire, ignore the rest.

6. 75 screenshots would have been very expensive to read one at a time. `montage` from ImageMagick tiled them into 5 grids at 15 per grid, readable in 5 image reads. Note the grids must be copied into a mounted folder (the outputs dir) because Read cannot reach `/tmp`.

## Learnings and discoveries

About Riaan (adding to the standing rules: sentence case, no em or en dashes, "Hurulab" one word, "lead designer", brevity when frustrated, tappable questions, mural means Miro):

- He reviews by screenshot. He will send a screenshot of a thing and say "remove this" or "irrelevant". Read the screenshot, make the exact change, do not ask what he meant.
- He iterates hard and fast on a deliverable. This questionnaire took about ten rounds. Do not defend a decision, just change it and confirm in one line.
- He hates preset answer options in a client-facing form. Every multi-select pill row was eventually stripped. All 15 questions are now plain text boxes. Default to free text.
- He wants category tags on questions, colour coded. He named brand, value proposition, pain points and newsletter, and asked for more suggestions. We added audience, proof, goals and assistant.
- "Are you sure?" and "check if anything is duplicated" mean run the real check, not a reassurance.
- When he says a thing is confusing, ask which part rather than rewriting blind. That question is still unanswered for the master prompt.
- Do not explain the cause of a problem he has already dismissed. When he said the findings file was not a cutoff, arguing about why was worthless. What mattered was that findings 3 to 5 were missing, and I could write them myself.

About the machinery:

- The Claude in Chrome extension has no filesystem access. It cannot write to the folder. Its output has to be pasted back, and long output can arrive truncated. Plan for that: ask it for one file at a time, or expect to reconstruct.
- Pasted markdown tables arrive flattened into one line and must be rebuilt.
- ImageMagick `montage` is available in the sandbox and is the way to read many screenshots cheaply. jsdom is installable via npm and is the way to actually test an HTML artifact's JavaScript rather than eyeballing it.
- WebPageTest json puts everything under `data.runs.1.firstView.steps[0]`, not the documented `median.firstView`. The per-request csv column named `image_save` is the image total, not the saving. The savings figure lives in the json as `image_savings` and in the optimisation screenshots. I nearly published the wrong number.

About Dada, the content:

- They are not a broker. They are a family investment company, founded in Tokyo in 2018 by Taiwanese investors, licence 東京都知事(1)第107915号, representative 馬瑞辰, that invests its own capital (自社資金による不動産投資) and also brokers. They carry the risk their buyers carry. No competitor in the field can say that. It is buried in one paragraph on their About page and it is probably the whole positioning.
- Taiwanese buyers are now roughly two thirds of foreign buyers of Tokyo 23-ward new condos. Dada is a Taiwanese-founded Tokyo firm ranking for nothing in any language.
- They have no analytics of any kind. No GA, no tag manager, no pixel. There is no traffic history to recover from anyone. Whatever we install is their first ever number.

## Architecture and design notes

The dada folder mirrors the Hurulab system with one change: one stakeholder means the three-layer answer system collapses to two (verbatim file, synthesis), and the cross-check that stakeholder disagreement used to provide now comes from evidence instead. Appendix A of the master prompt spells this out. When the client's belief and the research disagree, that gap is the finding, and it goes to Riaan rather than being quietly resolved.

The questionnaire's design constraints, learned the hard way today: free text only, no preset options, no jargon, one page, tags with colour, intro of one line, and every question must be answerable by a property person who has never thought about a website. The 15 cover goals (2), brand (4), pain points (2), value proposition (2), newsletter (2), audience (1), proof (1), assistant (1). The thin ones are audience, proof and assistant.
