Don't

Flood a page with amber, or use it as a background.

Use a semantic colour decoratively.

Use gradients, glows, or decorative shadows.

04 / space and grid

An eight-point grid

Every measure is a multiple of eight pixels. Text sits left, ragged right, on a column of 60 to 62 characters. Rhythm comes from varied spacing, tight inside a group and wide between groups, never the same gap everywhere.

8
16
24
40
64
96
  • Base unit 8px. Steps 8, 16, 24, 40, 64, 96. Large steps go fluid with clamp.
  • Section rhythm. Vertical padding clamp(2rem, 4vh, 3rem).
  • Reading column 60 to 62 characters. Layout max 64rem. Never full-bleed text.
  • Break the grid to mean something, not for decoration.

05 / layout and usage

Left-aligned, quiet, room to breathe

Do

Left-align. Asymmetric, editorial.

Let space carry the weight.

One idea per screen.

Real numbers, plainly set.

Don't

Centre everything, or wrap every block in a card.

Nest cards inside cards.

Add colour, glow, or gradient.

Fill space you can leave empty.
