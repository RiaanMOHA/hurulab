## The shorthand

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The board** | The owner's Miro board, transcribed into `research/positioning-board/` |
| **Knowns and bets** | The owner's own split between what is established and what is not. On the board, and the most useful page on it |
| **The Pixar seven-step** | A story template each project write-up originally carried. Those passages were removed: they read as history and were a writing exercise. Each file says where |
| **Open gaps** | A section in every project write-up where the author states what could not be proved. The most trustworthy content in `projects/` |
| **Discovery, or the discovery sprint** | The paid first engagement. What it costs, how long it runs and what the client keeps are all disputed. See `contradictions.md` |
| **接班** | Succession. A second or third generation owner taking over a family business, named as a target buyer |
| **The teaser demo** | A working piece built for a prospect before they commit. Whether it is free, and whether it is a link or a meeting, is disputed |
