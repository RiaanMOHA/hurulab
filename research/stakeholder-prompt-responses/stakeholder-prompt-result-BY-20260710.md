Hurulab stakeholder answers

Name and role: BY, team member.

1. What Hurulab is: A design and AI agency.
2. Plain words version: We use AI to build things.
3. If someone outside the industry asked: We use design and AI to build software and apps for businesses.
4. One big idea: Technology made human.
5. Big vision: To become a world-class studio.
6. Why it needs to exist: Good work is too slow. Great products take too long and cost too much, and we want to change that.
7. Origin story: Founders who knew each other. A small group who had worked together decided to start it.
8. Why it matters to me: I want to grow. This is a chance to learn, grow, and be part of something new.
9. Who it is for: Ambitious teams that care about quality. Not for those chasing quick, cheap work.
10. Their world before us: Bad past results. They tried before and got poor quality work they had to redo.
11. The hidden problem: They fear being fooled. They worry they will be sold hype and waste money again.
12. Real signals seen: Referrals. Clients have sent us to other people they know.
13. What is unique: Strong design and real AI skill in the same team.
14. What a happy client would say: The best team I have worked with.
15. A real moment: A demo that wowed. We showed a client the work and they were genuinely amazed.
16. AI role: We build software with AI, and we design the workflow of deterministic programs together with AI. We know how to identify the best way to do each part, together with good design.
17. Data or knowledge edge: Know-how on AI plus code. We know when to use AI and when to use normal code.
18. Repeatable work: A clear process. A set way of working we follow on every project.
19. AI trust: Prove it with results. Let the quality of the work earn their trust.
20. Culture: Work smart with the best, newest technology, understand it deeply, and use it to bring real value to our clients.
21. Good work, what we never ship: We never ship sloppy work, bad design, or hype. All of them.
22. How decisions get made: Fast and clear. Decide quickly with a clear owner, do not overthink.
23. The blend: People first, then tech. Start with what people need, then use design and tech to serve it.
24. Getting to root cause: Learn and write it down. Fix it, then note the lesson so it does not happen again.
25. Riskiest assumption: Clients will pay for this. That enough clients will pay well for design plus AI work.
26. Smallest test: A quick prototype. Build a fast demo to show what we can do.
27. Real signal it is working: All of them. Clients come back, clients refer us, and clients pay well.
28. Growth engine: All of them. Clients coming back, clients bringing other clients, and paid acquisition.
29. Rules we never break: All of them. Always be honest, never drop quality, and always respect people.
30. Kept in reserve: A few steady clients for steady income.
