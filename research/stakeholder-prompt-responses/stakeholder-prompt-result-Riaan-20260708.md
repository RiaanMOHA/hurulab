Hurulab stakeholder answers

Name and role: Riaan, lead designer.

1. What Hurulab is: A new kind of agency that combines AI, tech and design to create products or services for companies, big and small. It is not only good design taste and user experience, we also go into your business, understand everything about it, then train your staff to use these workflows, so that once we hand off you are ready to take it from there. You basically only need us once.
2. Plain words version: All of them together: we build your product, we help you actually put AI to work, and we understand your business, build the thing, and train your team to run it themselves.
3. The one big idea: After we are done, we become useless, because we have given you everything you need to continue by yourself. We do not just give you the product, we give you the tools and the training to keep doing it.
4. The audacious version: The go-to agency for every company, big and small, in Taiwan.
5. The question or itch: All of these, why so much capital is wasted, why vendors just take the order without understanding the business, and why good design is so rare here. Also why companies find it so hard to scale outside Taiwan while keeping global standards for design and tech. And to not just hand over an Excel file of what you want, but guide you through the whole process.
6. Origin story: It is starting right now. We saw there might be a need in the market, and it is beginning now, with the first case study being Jadegia.
7. Why it matters personally: I like solving real problems.
8. Who it is for, and not for: For companies that are sick of working with other agencies that cost a lot of money, take a lot of time, and do not really give solutions. Not for traditional companies that will not accept change.
9. The client's world before: Burned by slow, costly agencies, and lacking the skills or scale to meet their goals within their own constraints.
10. The unspoken problem: They do not know what they need.
11. Real actions from clients: Jadegia is almost live, and there is genuine interest. I have done my own research and found actual people who need this.
12. What only Hurulab offers: Business, design, and tech in one team that understands the business and builds the thing, at speed, and you can already use the product before you pay for it.
13. What a happy client would say: They understood us fast.
14. A real moment or scene: Using it before paying, tapping through your own working product early on.
15. How central AI is: One of the main differentiators is that we actually know how to use it, how to train people to use it, and how to build things with it. And we use taste in design to make it not look like AI slop.
16. Knowledge or data advantage: Our way of working plus tool mastery, and design taste on top of AI so it does not look like AI.
17. Making it repeatable: Both, codify the process and reuse scalable tools, tech, and artefacts.
18. How clients should trust the AI: A difficult one. We will definitely use case studies, but we can also show results really quickly without much effort, and we can understand their business very well with the tools we have.
19. Culture: Not answered. Riaan said this is not really an important question.
20. What good work means, never ship: Never ship anything that misses the goal, and never ship sloppy or slop-looking work.
21. How decisions get made: Fast and evidence-led.
22. The blend of design, tech, human: Understand the problem first, then build, with design taste holding it together so the tech and AI feel human, not generic.
23. Getting to the root of problems: Ask why until the real cause shows.
24. Riskiest assumption: That clients will pay for this.
25. Smallest testable version: A quick working demo.
26. The real signal it is working: First, if we can use our strategy to successfully cold call or message potential clients on LinkedIn, 104, wherever. Then, if we can close contracts faster because we can explain what we do and show how efficient we are and everything we can do, for a better price than anyone else. And best of all, word of mouth, clients actually recommending us and telling other people about us.
27. Growth engine: Clients bringing other clients.
28. Rules never to break: Never ship slop.
29. Reserve against a bad month: Not answered. Riaan said this is not important at all.
