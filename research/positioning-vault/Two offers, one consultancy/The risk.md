# Two offers, one consultancy — the risk

*(Online / sovereign platforms)*

**Cross-cutting risks of doing both:** over-committing and failing at both; one small team's capacity contended across two very different work types; the context-switching cost of very different complexity; and positioning dilution, since 'we do both' is a weaker market story than one, sharp, message.
