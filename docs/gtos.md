## `trust`
a glass box, not a black box

Every claim clicks back to its source. Every prediction is frozen before we act and graded after. The system can never lie about whether it was right.

## `reliability`
structural, not probabilistic

Facts live in a ledger, never in a model. Anything with one correct answer goes to code. Accounts don't hallucinate, because accounts never pass through a model.

## `efficiency`
every cent has a receipt

Every task routes to the cheapest engine that answers correctly. Every cost is logged. The routing itself is graded, so cost per outcome falls every round.

## the cycle

how the three are manufactured
seven-step cycle, each step marked with the value it produces

Follow one engagement top to bottom. Each step shows what happens, who is on duty, and, in the amber box, what it produces for you.

### 01 — sync
`reliability`
sync, starting from your authoritative records

Your authoritative records, ledger, crm, erp, logs, flow in continuously. Nothing is overwritten; every change is versioned, every mismatch flagged.

> **on duty · reconciler**
> Keeps our picture of your business honest against your own books, every day, not once at setup.
>
> **what this buys you**
> The system never quietly drifts away from your reality.

### 02 — structure
`trust` `reliability`
structure, flat data becomes knowledge that can answer questions

Flat records become a typed map of your business: entities, derived signals, possible actions. Every fact carries its source and is labeled, verified or estimated. The two are never mixed.

> **on duty · ontology**
> The one place facts live. Ai reads from it; ai never writes fiction into it.
>
> **what this buys you**
> Answers grounded in your data, not in a model's imagination.

### 03 — hypothesize
`trust`
hypothesize, seal the prediction first, then act

A problem surfaces in your facts. Together we project the solution and its expected outcome, and freeze that prediction before acting. It cannot be edited afterward.

> **on duty · advisor**
> Your bespoke counterpart: understands your intent fast, pulls the evidence, and stays skeptical, ideas that can't find an audience get flagged, not flattered. Go / widen / no-go, with the list attached.
>
> **what this buys you**
> You spend your time on insights, not on learning our system. And you never get sold a plan the data doesn't support.

### 04 — route
`efficiency`
route, send the right task to the cheapest reliable brain

The plan breaks into tasks. Each routes down a ladder, rule → embedding → local model → frontier model → human, always the cheapest engine that answers correctly. One-correct-answer tasks go to code, never a model.

> **on duty · router**
> Logs every decision with its cost and confidence. You can audit which intelligence touched which task, and what it cost. Budget caps act as circuit breakers, spend can never run away silently.
>
> **what this buys you**
> You never overpay for api calls a script could do, and you can never burn a year's budget in a month. Your ai spend becomes a metered line item you can read.

### 05 — build & execute
`trust`
execute, do the work, with records kept the whole way

The solution is built and run inside your real workflow, not a pilot on mock data. The attempt itself becomes a record: what was tried, in what order, at what cost.

> **on duty · explainer** — active at every step
> Attaches a receipt to every result: which engines touched it, which facts grounded it, which sources back those facts.
>
> **what this buys you**
> Every answer comes with its provenance. Nothing asks to be taken on faith.

### 06 — verify → ground truth
`trust` `reliability`
reconcile and post, reality arrives and is checked against the sealed prediction

Reality arrives. The outcome is reconciled against the frozen prediction and scored. Only then does the entry pass the gate into the ground truth log, append-only, never rewritten. This is the only door between hypothesis and fact.

> **on duty · grader** — sole key to the gate
> Nobody, no model, no engineer, no deadline, can promote a guess into a fact. Only graded reality passes.
>
> **what this buys you**
> You always know what's proven and what's projected. Results can never quietly become premises.

### 07 — compound
`efficiency` `trust` `reliability`
compound, why the system gets more accurate the more it's used

The graded ledger becomes study material: which hypotheses held, which tools earned their cost, which patterns repeat. The deltas reweight the signals and sharpen the next round, and our own machinery is graded by the same standard.

> **on duty · grader** · second scope
> We grade ourselves with the same ledger we grade your predictions, routing efficiency, coverage, plan quality included.
>
> **what this buys you**
> A system that is measurably better this quarter than last, with the scorecard to prove it.
