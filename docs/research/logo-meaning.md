# The meaning behind the mark

Owner ruling, 17 August 2026: the logomark must carry meaning. The research in `logomark.md`
places that meaning in the brief, where it steers appropriateness and specificity; the viewer
is never required to decode it. This file names the true things about hurulab a mark may
embody. Every claim traces to `docs/evidence.md` or `docs/design-rules/brand.md`, and the
owner's proven-versus-bet split is respected: an intention is labeled an intention. This file
arranges the brand foundation for the logo work and decides nothing; where it and `brand.md`
disagree, brand.md wins.

## Owner ruling, 17 August 2026

The owner judged the four meanings for the mark, in their own words: 1 is okay, 2 is
completely wrong, 3 is not really correct, 4 is fine. So the mark stands on meaning 1, may
use meaning 4, and does not use 2 or 3. The ruling is about the logo only: 2 and 3 remain
true of the brand and stand unchanged in `brand.md`.

## The meanings a mark may stand on

1. **One team that understands your business before it builds.** The one claim all five
   people make unprompted, to two different questions: "business, design and technology in
   one team that understands the business before it builds." (Q2 and Q13, all five) The
   contrast that sharpens it: vendors who "just take the order without understanding the
   business" (Q6, Riaan); "They just take the order and build." (Q6, Cal)

2. **Use it before you pay for it.** The single idea, settled 30 July 2026: "companies waste
   capital because they buy software they have never used. So you use it before you pay for
   it." (brand.md section 7)

3. **Evidence over trust, numbers forward.** The buyer this serves "fear[s] being fooled,
   sold hype and made to waste money again" (Q11, BY), and the design consequence is "sober
   and demonstrative rather than persuasive, numbers forward" (brand.md section 7).

4. **The honest stop.** The pricing plan's discovery rule: if it should not be built, deliver
   the report, keep the fee, say so honestly. (docs/evidence.md part 8, from funnel-pricing)

## The intentions, which may only be worn as promises

- **The handover.** "Once we are done we become useless, because we have given you everything
  to continue on your own." (Q4, Riaan) No client has yet run alone after a handover, so this
  is publishable as intention only. (brand.md sections 7 and 11; evidence part 2)

## The most ownable meanings, by the research's own test

The blanding research says the escape from generic is a specific, true, even unflattering
characteristic that no competitor claims. hurulab has two:

- **We tell you not to build**, and the fee stands (meaning 4). The rule is proven as stated
  policy; no outcome of it has been measured. A mark built on the honest stop celebrates a
  moment other vendors hide.
- **We work to make ourselves useless** (the handover, an intention). Stronger as a story,
  weaker as evidence; if the mark stands on this, the site must state it as an intention.

## What no meaning may claim

The owner's own limits, from the Miro board (evidence part 2): no cold win, thin build-depth
proof, no measured outcome, and no client running alone yet. A mark's meaning may not imply
any of these.

## The open question this file does not decide

Whether "judge it before you commit" amends the single idea is an open owner decision in
`PLAN.md`. Until ruled on, meaning 2 stands as written in brand.md.
