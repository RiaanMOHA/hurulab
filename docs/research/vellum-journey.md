\# Reference observation

\#\# 1\. Journey mechanics

The homepage is a single vertical page moved by scrolling. Nothing on the homepage requires a click to reveal the next section; scrolling is the primary advance mechanism. Several sections play their own self-running animations on a loop regardless of scroll (the hero chat, the skills feed). A click-through flow exists separately and is entered from the hero.

Order of the homepage as the reader scrolls: a light hero with a self-playing chat, a pink "always on" band with a tab switcher, a light "I handle everything you do" band with a running skills feed and a skill list, a light band with a mascot cluster and a "10x yourself" line, a light comparison table, a light-gradient plugins card, and finally a dark closing call-to-action followed by a dark footer.

The click-through flow: clicking the hero's "MEET YOUR AI" button opens a dedicated screen at \`/account/signup\`. This flow is a single onboarding screen rather than a multi-screen wizard, described below. Advancing from it (via "Continue") leads to an external account-creation wall.

Click-through flow, screen by screen:

Screen 1 — Onboarding screen (URL \`/account/signup\`). Headline verbatim: "Meet your new" on line one and a rotating second line that cycled through (verbatim) "Personal Intelligence.", "GTM Engineer.", "Finance Ops" (partially transitioning), and "Product Lead." Subtext verbatim: "The most powerful assistant that can handle your work and life admin tasks." No text input or choice is requested to advance; a right-hand panel auto-cycles through product demos on its own. Input required to continue: none on this screen. Progress indicator: none observed. Buttons/links present, labels verbatim: "Continue", "Sign in" (after "Already have an account?"), and "Download for macOS". The right panel cycled through several self-playing demos: an avatar customizer (with "Back", a close "X", a mascot preview, "Generate Random", three arrow-based selector rows, "Discard", and "Confirm", plus a bottom row of colored mascots), a multi-channel chat demo ("Accessing via Telegram", "How can I interact with you?", channel chips Desktop / Telegram / Slack / Whatsapp / Phone / CLI, and a Telegram window labeled "Ash bot"), an integration demo (mascot eyes beside a Slack logo on an orange field), and a chat-input demo (a purple flower mascot above an "Ask me anything" bar with attachment, mic, and voice icons). How this screen hands over to the next: pressing "Continue" navigates the whole page to an external sign-up screen; the left column stays fixed while only the right panel's demos swap. The demo swaps were observed as pieces flying apart/assembling (mascot eyes and body fragments scattering on a colored field) rather than a single slide.

Account/payment wall: "Continue" led to a sign-up page on a different domain (\`login.platform.vellum.ai/sign-up\`). Per the task, I recorded that this is an account-creation wall and stopped there without entering data or creating an account.

Back path: within the onboarding screen's avatar-customizer demo a "Back" link and a close "X" were visible. To leave the onboarding screen itself, only the browser back control was available (no on-page back button to the homepage was observed). Could not determine whether the sign-up wall offered its own back path (not entered).

\#\# 2\. Storytelling

The copy speaks in the first person as the assistant/product ("I'm your…", "I handle everything you do", "I'm always on", "I'll help you…", "Make AI finally work for you"). The reader is addressed as "you". The hero frames a morning wake-up: the reader sends "wake up" and the assistant introduces itself.

At least five headlines, verbatim, in order: "Morning, I'm your" (with a rotating role such as "Marketer.", "Email Assistant.", "Engineer.", "Household Manager.", "Travel Assistant."), then "I'm always on, everywhere you are", then "I handle everything you do", then "I'll help you become AI-native and 10x yourself", then "See how I compare", then "Plugins give your assistant superpowers", then "Make AI finally work for you".

Narrative arc, each section in order, one line each. Hero: a chat where the assistant wakes, names its role, and says it will "handle the busywork" — establishes the assistant as personal and ready. "I'm always on, everywhere you are": presents ubiquity across channels — makes the case it is always reachable. "I handle everything you do": lists concrete skills with a live activity feed — proves breadth of autonomous work. Mascot-cluster line "I'll help you become AI-native and 10x yourself": an aspirational payoff for the reader — states the benefit. "See how I compare": a competitor table — positions the product against alternatives. "Plugins give your assistant superpowers": an extensibility pitch with categories — shows it can grow. "Make AI finally work for you": closing call to action — asks for signup.

Places the experience shows the product doing something rather than describing it: the hero plays a live chat with a "wake up" bubble and timestamped reply, an animated status card (e.g. "Sorting Inbox | Drafting replies and flagging priorities | Gmail", "Checking the Funnel | Finding what's driving traffic and signups | Google Analytics", "Checking the Build | Reviewing PRs & issues | GitHub", "Running the House | Planning errands and appointments | Google Calendar", "Planning Your Trip | Finding flights, hotels, and activities | Google Flights"). The "I handle everything you do" section runs a scrolling console feed of skill log lines (e.g. entries tagged SKILL·EM01 through SKILL·TR08) with a rotating highlighted card ("RESEARCH / Compiled a brief on the Series B prospect…", "TRAVEL / Booked your SFO→JFK flight…", "COMMS / Three Slack threads blew up while you were in deep work…"), while a synced list highlights the active skill (Email Triage, Calendar, Meeting Prep, Delegation, Research, Comms, Project Tracking, Travel). The onboarding right panel demonstrates the product surfaces (avatar builder, Telegram/multi-channel chat, Slack integration, ask-anything input). The "always on" tabs each swap in demo copy for Slack, pocket, and meetings.

Reader input echoed back later: within the hero fiction the assistant echoes the reader's "wake up" and replies as if to that person, and the copy repeatedly reflects "your voice, your priorities, and your routines". No place was observed where the reader's own typed input is captured and replayed later (no free-text field was completed).

\#\# 3\. Composition

Screens tend to carry one idea each, centered. The hero splits into a left-aligned chat column; most later sections are single centered columns (headline, one line of support, one link/button). The comparison table is the densest, multi-column block. The plugins block is a single wide card with content left-weighted and mascots tucked into corners. Generous empty space surrounds the centered headlines throughout.

Background rhythm, in order and by role: the hero sits on a plain light field (calm, sets the stage). It is followed by a saturated colored band for "always on" (a bright accent moment that raises energy and signals a distinct promise). The site returns to plain light for the skills section and the mascot-cluster/comparison/plugins sequence (workmanlike, evidence-and-detail mode). It closes on a dark band for the final call to action and footer (a settling, high-contrast finish). The onboarding flow lives on its own dark field, with the animated demos held inside a bright card.

Recurring personality devices: a blob mascot with two large expressive eyes recurs throughout, appearing in different colors (pink in the hero, orange in onboarding, and a whole family of colored variants — pink, red, green, yellow, purple, teal, flower-shaped and spiky forms — in the mascot cluster and plugins corners). The mascots frequently "peek" from the edges of bands (large eyes rising from the bottom of the pink section, and a row of characters peeking up from the divider above the footer). In the onboarding hero the orange mascot's eyes appeared to track the cursor. The mascot also marks the active row in the skills list (a mascot peeks from the right edge of the highlighted row).

Interactive pieces and their states: the hero's primary button reads "MEET YOUR AI" (green pill, arrow prefix); on hover it appeared slightly larger/lighter and on click it navigated to the onboarding screen. The "always on" tab switcher shows three tabs ("In Slack", "In your pocket", "In your meetings") as a pill group with a white selected pill; clicking a tab slides the white pill to it and swaps the supporting copy. The skills list rows sit as light bordered cards at rest; the active row turns to a dark fill with light text and a mascot peeking at its right edge (the highlight auto-advances). The plugin category chips ("Productivity 11", "Developer 5", "Lifestyle 5", "Hobby 4", "Marketing 4") sit flat at rest and gain a raised/lighter background on hover. The header "Product" control opens a dropdown of three items with icon, title, and description ("Overview / See everything Vellum can do", "For Business / AI that works for your whole team", "Personal AI / Your own AI, built around you"). "Resources" and "Compare" are also dropdown buttons (contents not opened).
