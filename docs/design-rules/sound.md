# Sound

Owner decision, 5 August 2026. This file owns interface sound and nothing else.

---

## 1. The decision

**The site will use sound.** The owner's direction, stated for pro-360 and applied here: sound
is part of how the experience hooks people and treats them well. The reference is the owner's
own: *Designing with Sound: Fundamentals for Products and Services*, Amber Case and Aaron Day,
O'Reilly 2018.

## 2. When it gets designed

**Against real pages doing real work, never before.** A sound designed against an imaginary
moment is a guess. Sound design starts once the demo website's real screens exist, and the
owner verifies every sound by listening, at real volume. **Doing nothing stays a real
outcome** for any given moment, or the research is decoration.

## 3. The web constraints

From the archived Apple research (`.thoughts/apple-hig-research-2026-08-04.md` section 4):
browsers block autoplaying sound, so sound on the web is user-initiated. Any sound shipped is
feedback, never decoration, never the only carrier of meaning, always disableable with a
visible control.

## 4. Not decided

Which moments carry sound, what the sounds are, and their volumes. Open until the real
screens exist. Recording a guess here is how errors start.
