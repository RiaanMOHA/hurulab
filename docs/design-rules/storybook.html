Foundations · Spacing

Only steps with a job. The parent owns the gap.

Foundations · Motion

Fast in, faster out. Nothing exceeds 450ms.

Enter cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) for anything appearing, any state change, any hover. Exit cubic-bezier(0.6, 0, 0.8, 0.6) for anything leaving, always shorter than the entry. Only transform and opacity animate. Reduced motion means off, not slowed. Rebuilt 5 August 2026 on the archived research. motion.md.

Hover a chip to feel its speed. The leaving chip runs backwards on its own curve: it empties.
