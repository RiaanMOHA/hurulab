## The problem

Why you hesitate before a big technology project

1. **The price you paid was more than you were told.** You know it from your last project. The
   number you signed is never the number you paid.
2. **Nothing says it will meet your goal.** A proposal can't tell you whether the build will
   meet your goal. You find out after what you paid for has been delivered.
3. **You get something, and it isn't what you asked for.** A misunderstood requirement,
   complexity nobody saw, an expense underestimated. You end up with something that fits a
   plan but doesn't fit your customer.

These risks come from deciding before you have the evidence. Discovery brings you the
evidence first.

## What we do

Discovery

Discovery starts with a few free sessions with the people closest to your problem. We play the
problem back to you, tie it to your goals, and agree the one problem most worth solving. If
you go ahead, the next part is paid: a plan with a fixed end date, usually a few weeks, and you
see the quote before it starts.

Discovery then gives you the evidence to decide before you commit. A prototype you can use, a
technology and security audit of a legacy platform, or a strategy to pull together your
branding, design and user experience. Either way you leave with a realistic, evidence-based
estimate of what it takes to reach your goal, and you choose the approach that fits. And if
you don't need AI, we'll tell you that, too.

## The team

What we bring

Here's what the team can do, once Discovery shows what you need. We're a multidisciplinary
team. Everyone on the team uses AI in their daily work, and we've built our own products with
it. A developer no longer only writes code, and a designer makes more than a logo. Nobody here
is tied to one skill, so teams stay small and you talk to the people doing the work.

1. **Discovery.** Discovery is how every engagement opens, and it's what sets us apart from a
   standard consultancy. We spend a few weeks understanding the problem and the goal before
   anything gets built. You learn up front, so less gets built twice and less goes to waste.
2. **The day we come to see you.** We call this our "espresso bar" moment. We gather round
   what we've done, play with a prototype, and talk ideas. You see how a solution could look
   and work without us building the whole thing, and you make the call for your company, your
   teams and your clients.
3. **Building software.** Software engineering is what we do. Our engineers use AI every day,
   so the repetitive work is handled and their time goes on the hard parts. We're tied to no
   particular technology. We use the one that best fits what needs building.
4. **Automating repetitive work.** We look at the repetitive tasks and the manual processing
   in your business and find safe ways to hand them to software. Quality goes up, and your
   team's time goes on the problems only people can solve.
5. **Websites and online presence.** A new site, a refresh, a tighter fit with your brand, a
   cleaner user experience, or localisation. Our design and user experience team can give an
   old site a new look, or a current one the functionality it needs to grow your business.
6. **Answers from your data.** The insight you need to make a decision, without anyone writing
   a complicated query. Collection, analysis and the report on your desk.
7. **Considered AI.** If your data can't leave the building, we'll show you what running AI in
   house costs today and whether it's worth it. Either way, you get the benefit of AI without
   the hidden costs, trained for quality and accurate answers.
8. **Training your team on their own work.** Your staff already use AI, mostly as a search
   engine. We train them on their actual tasks, so they know which parts of the job it can
   take on.
9. **Ongoing support.** New AI models or tools come out every week, and your business can keep
   improving with them. We stay on top of it. When there's something new and useful, we can
   let you know.

## Example use case

It's a coffee shop. How much can technology really help?

Your business may not be a coffee company, but you have suppliers, staff, customers, stock,
branding and marketing. You might be a manufacturer selling through distributors that wants to
reach buyers directly. Or a brand that sells well in its stores and wants its website to sell
as well, at home and abroad.

[Placeholder. A real client case replaces this before launch.] The client is a small coffee
shop. They have 3 locations and a roastery, with about 30 staff. They sell in the shops,
online, and wholesale.

First, discovery. We ask a lot of questions

- What do you do every week that you wish you didn't have to?
- Where do mistakes happen most?
- Where do you lose the most money?
- What does the owner do that nobody else can do, or nobody else has the time to do?
- What do customers ask over and over?
- What decisions are made on gut feel?
- Where are you struggling to meet your ambition for the business?

And here's what we might find

1. The owner spends Sunday evening on staff scheduling. Past data and this week's events can
   predict the schedule for the week ahead. The owner reviews and approves it in ten minutes.
2. The company orders beans on a schedule, or when the beans run out. Bean prices and stock
   can be tracked together, with a nudge to the owner when it's the right time to buy and
   enough stock is on hand.
3. Questions come in by email in English and Japanese. The website has the answers, but only
   in Mandarin. It can be localised in a day or two, with a native speaker checking it.
4. Customers comment or complain about certain batches. The feedback can be analysed and fed
   back to the roasters, so the batches get more consistent.

What discovery delivers

During discovery we build working demos for the staff scheduling and the wholesale price
tracking in a few days, and show the team how to collect customer feedback and read it. Then
we spend a day on site walking the owner through all of it.

If the company wants to go on, we build the tools. Or they take our plan to another team, or
build it in house. Either way they made the decision with the evidence in front of them.

Every business is different, and we'll help you find what's worth doing in yours.
