Purple, the brand

Hue 318 still, but dust leaves it a mauve. Chroma tops out at 0.062 against proposal 1's 0.152.

300 fills, 600 draws. Purple 300 is a fill only; purple 600 is text only.

Orange, the accent

Sand, not gold. At this dust load extinction stops depending on wavelength, so the glow loses its color rather than deepening.

Gold fills, it never writes. It carries the ink at 11.64:1. Pale and high, where proposal 1's sits deep and hot.

The neutrals

Warm dun at the top, cooling to slate at depth. The cool end is what stops it reading as a flat brown.
