Hurulab is a Taipei technology team for established businesses with a goal to hit. We work with the people closest to your problem to find out what's worth building. Your goal comes before the solution.

What we bring

Here's what the team can do, once Discovery shows what you need. We're a multidisciplinary team. Everyone on the team uses AI in their daily work, and we've built our own products with it. A developer no longer only writes code, and a designer makes more than a logo. Nobody here is tied to one skill, so teams stay small and you talk to the people doing the work.

  1. 1

    Discovery.

    Discovery is how every engagement opens, and it's what sets us apart from a standard consultancy. We spend a few weeks understanding the problem and the goal before anything gets built. You learn up front, so less gets built twice and less goes to waste.

  2. 2

    The day we come to see you.

    We call this our "espresso bar" moment. We gather round what we've done, play with a prototype, and talk ideas. You see how a solution could look and work without us building the whole thing, and you make the call for your company, your teams and your clients.

  3. 3

    Building software.

    Software engineering is what we do. Our engineers use AI every day, so the repetitive work is handled and their time goes on the hard parts. We're tied to no particular technology. We use the one that best fits what needs building.

  4. 4

    Automating repetitive work.

    We look at the repetitive tasks and the manual processing in your business and find safe ways to hand them to software. Quality goes up, and your team's time goes on the problems only people can solve.

  5. 5

    Websites and online presence.

    A new site, a refresh, a tighter fit with your brand, a cleaner user experience, or localisation. Our design and user experience team can give an old site a new look, or a current one the functionality it needs to grow your business.

  6. 6

    Answers from your data.

    The insight you need to make a decision, without anyone writing a complicated query. Collection, analysis and the report on your desk.

  7. 7

    Considered AI.

    If your data can't leave the building, we'll show you what running AI in house costs today and whether it's worth it. Either way, you get the benefit of AI without the hidden costs, trained for quality and accurate answers.

  8. 8

    Training your team on their own work.

    Your staff already use AI, mostly as a search engine. We train them on their actual tasks, so they know which parts of the job it can take on.

  9. 9

    Ongoing support.

    New AI models or tools come out every week, and your business can keep improving with them. We stay on top of it. When there's something new and useful, we can let you know.

Discovery

Discovery starts with a few free sessions with the people closest to your problem. We play the problem back to you, tie it to your goals, and agree the one problem most worth solving. If you go ahead, the next part is paid: a plan with a fixed end date, usually a few weeks, and you see the quote before it starts.

Discovery then gives you the evidence to decide before you commit. A prototype you can use, a technology and security audit of a legacy platform, or a strategy to pull together your branding, design and user experience. Either way you leave with a realistic, evidence-based estimate of what it takes to reach your goal, and you choose the approach that fits. And if you don't need AI, we'll tell you that, too.

Why you hesitate before a big technology project

  1. 1

    The price you paid was more than you were told.

    You know it from your last project. The number you signed is never the number you paid.

  2. 2

    Nothing says it will meet your goal.

    A proposal can't tell you whether the build will meet your goal. You find out after what you paid for has been delivered.

  3. 3

    You get something, and it isn't what you asked for.

    A misunderstood requirement, complexity nobody saw, an expense underestimated. You end up with something that fits a plan but doesn't fit your customer.

These risks come from deciding before you have the evidence. Discovery brings you the evidence first.
