Six marks, drawn at the sizes they have to survive.

Each one is shown alone and in the lockup, from hero size down to a 16 pixel icon, on white and on black. Press play on any card to see its motion. The two rules that apply to all six: the mark is the brand purple, and it sits left of the name.

My reading, and it is only a starting point.

Two are worth drawing properly. Four are not.

Three arms and the open-core asterisk are the only two whose shape carries an idea hurulab can defend: parts becoming one team, and a centre nothing unproven gets through. Three overlapping and Out of the frame both depend on transparency and both die as icons. First, filled reads as a progress bar. Six spokes is the safest and the emptiest, because a plain asterisk still means footnote.

My reading, not a decision
Argue with all of it

The marks are the same shapes you saw before. What is new is seeing them at 16 pixels, on black, and moving.
