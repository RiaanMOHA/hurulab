Your experience with AI today

  1. 01

    AI is just a search engine.

    You open a chat, type a question, and read the answer. It's Google, just better. But AI can do much, much more. In most cases, you can bring AI inside your workflow, to automate it and save you time and money.

  2. 02

    AI is another task I need to remember.

    AI can do many things on its own. You won't need to lift a finger! It can review contracts as soon as they're sent in, or draft daily reports before you wake up. If AI isn't taking tasks off your plate, you might be using it wrong.

  3. 03

    Everyone says I definitely need AI!

    Maybe. But maybe not. Not every problem needs AI, and AI can't do everything (at least, not yet). We'll show you which parts of your business can use it, and which can't.

The greatest luxury is having your time back to think and focus on what matters most.

Example use case:

The client (not a real client) is a small espresso bar. They have 3 locations and a roastery, with about 30 staff. They sell in the shops, online, and wholesale.

It's a coffee shop. How much can AI really help?

First, we ask a bunch of questions:

  • What do you do every week that you wish you didn't have to?
  • Where do mistakes happen most?
  • Where do you lose the most money?
  • What does the owner do that nobody else can do, or nobody else has the time to do?
  • What do customers ask over and over?
  • What decisions are made on gut feel?

And here's what we might find:

  • The owner spends Sunday evening on staff scheduling. AI can use historical data and current events to track and predict scheduling needs for the next week. The owner reviews and approves in 10 minutes.
  • The company orders beans on a schedule, or when the beans run out. AI can track bean prices and inventory, and notify the owner when it's the best time to buy while keeping enough stock on hand.
  • Questions come in via email in English and Japanese. The website has the answers, but it's only in Mandarin. AI can localize the website in a day or two. We still recommend a native speaker review.
  • Customers comment or complain about certain batches. AI can analyze the feedback and help the roasters fine tune their process to improve consistency.

During the initial discovery phase, we'll build out proof-of-concept demos for staff scheduling and wholesale price tracking software in just a few days. We'll also show the team how to keep track of customer feedback and use AI to analyze it.

If the company wants to move forward, we can build the tools for them directly. Or they can find another development team, or use AI to build it in-house.

We know your business isn't a coffee company, but you have suppliers, staff, customers, inventory, and finances, too. Every business is different, and we'll help you find the best use of AI for yours.
