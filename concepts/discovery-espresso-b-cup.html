First call, 30 minutes

With the person closest to the problem, deliberately not the decision maker. We get the problem out fast and agree roughly what success would look like.

We go away and build a pack

Their problem in our words, a goal statement, and visual proof, this could be a simple prototype, screenshots, aim is explain the problem back to them as we've understood it

The longer 1 hour session.

We present the above, adjust based on feedback but be clear that solutions are easy once the problem is understand alignment has been achieved on the goal. If they are unsure about the goal, we can walk them through options based on the problem (e.g the goal is retention)

We then spec, size and quote.

If anything has since changed, we down tools and don't proceed until they have gotten their own house in order)

If the above is accepted, we move straight in to the paid discovery

More detailed scope / Setting up the project / Enviros etc

The sprint itself

Working towards the 1 specific, well-defined problem. We engage who needs to be brought in and when

The closing day

We spend half a day to a full day walking through everything we built, with the decision maker present. They see the results, how to measure success against the goal etc.

Following from this, they might want to build out further, continuing in to a project that with a broader scope or just take what we've delivered and leave it there.
