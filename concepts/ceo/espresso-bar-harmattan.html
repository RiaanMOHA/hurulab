Core concept

Shift left. All of it.

Taiwanese enterprises are pragmatically risk-averse. The one-day format works because every source of technical friction is eliminated before anyone walks into the room, so day one is reserved exclusively for the client’s domain experts doing visible, valuable work.

Data drop kit · due with the T-7 handover

The bean bag

The data never lives in one place. It is scattered across inboxes, chat threads, ERP screens, binders, and someone’s phone. The bean bag is a fixed drop structure the client fills without integrating anything: they collect the beans, Hurulab does the roasting, grinding, and pulling.

The drop structure and its house rules

Six folders and a manifest. Nothing else.

espresso-drop/ ├─ 00_MANIFEST.xlsx one row per file ├─ 01_documents/ invoices, quotes ├─ 02_system-exports/ ERP tables, raw CSV ├─ 03_reference/ tolerances, masters ├─ 04_comms/ emails, chats ├─ 05_odds-and-ends/ photos, scans, notes └─ 06_golden-cases/ 10 clean + 10 nightmares
Do not clean anything.

Cleaning is Hurulab’s job. Helpful pre-editing destroys the signal the ontology mapping needs.

Raw formats only.

Original file types. No PDFs of spreadsheets, no retyped tables. A screenshot is fine only when the screen is the only form the data takes.

One drop point.

Everything lands in the shared folder. Nothing arrives as an email attachment to a Hurulab inbox.

Forward, do not format.

Relevant email and chat threads are exported as they are into 04_comms, never copy-pasted into a document.

When unsure, include it, flagged in the manifest.

The isolated sandbox and access control exist precisely so inclusion is safe.

STEP 1 · BY T-12Name a data runner

One person collects; everyone else just forwards to them. Usually the IT lead’s delegate, not necessarily technical.

STEP 2 · T-10Concierge call, 30 minutes

A Hurulab engineer walks the runner through the bean bag folder by folder and agrees the export list and date range per system.

STEP 3Export, do not filter

ERP, CRM and MES tables leave as raw CSV with all columns for the agreed period. Filtering hides exactly the edge cases the day needs.

STEP 4Photograph the rest

Paper binders, whiteboards, the tolerance sheet living on one laptop. Phone photos into 05_odds-and-ends are enough.

STEP 5Log it in the manifest

One row per file as it is dropped: source, owner, period, why it matters, personal data flag. Ten seconds per file, and it becomes Hurulab’s cleaning map.

The engineered climax · 16:30

The unscripted stress test

Taiwanese business culture runs on top-down executive authority: the general manager, chairman, or division VP signs, or nothing moves. So the final 90 minutes are engineered for exactly one outcome: the executive personally breaks the script and watches the system hold.

01Hand over

The CEO or general manager is invited to hand the operator a completely unscripted, highly complex document or dataset, on the spot.

02Upload live

The client operator uploads it into the system in front of the room. No rehearsal, no safety net, and none needed.

03Watch it execute

Instant reasoning, full audit trails, and a real writeback, performed by the executive’s own staff.

The executive sets the test themselves. That is what makes it evidence rather than a demo, and it is the only reason the signature comes the same week.
