Corner scale, three ladders

The same pieces in each column: a tile holding a dark card holding a panel, then a button, a tag, an input and a checkbox. Every corner takes the C2 curve, capsules included. Pick by eye.

Ladder one, strictly Apple

Buttons, tags and inputs are capsules, the way Apple's touch layouts work. Cards sit at 1.5rem. Panels are concentric: the card's corner minus the padding. Tiles are 2rem on mobile easing to 1.5rem on desktop.

Nothing is eyeballed.

Ladder three, concentric spine
