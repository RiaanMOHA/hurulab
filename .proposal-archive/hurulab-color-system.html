Hurulab · colour system

Purple sunrise

Two flat colours. Violet for the sky before dawn, coral for first light. No gradients.

Primary — violet

The main brand colour, in three shades: one for light backgrounds, one for dark, one deep for text.

The brand violet
Buttons, key marks, the colour people remember. #7C3AED
Deep violet
Headings and text on light backgrounds. #5B21B6
Light violet
The violet for dark backgrounds. #A78BFA

Secondary — coral

First light. The warm accent, in three shades for the same light, dark and text jobs.

The brand coral
Accents and highlights. Dark text sits on top of it. #FF6B4A
Light coral
The coral for dark backgrounds. #FF9E80
Deep coral
Coral deep enough to use as text on light. #C63410

Neutrals

Eleven greys with a whisper of violet so they never look off-brand. Lightest is a near-white, darkest a near-black. Neither is pure.

For the developers

Tokens. Two colours, three shades each, plus the neutrals.

Design tokens (CSS) 
:root{
  /* primary — violet */
  --purple-400:#A78BFA; --purple-500:#7C3AED; --purple-700:#5B21B6;
  /* secondary — coral */
  --coral-400:#FF9E80; --coral-500:#FF6B4A; --coral-700:#C63410;
  /* violet-tinted neutrals */
  --neutral-50:#F7F5FC;  --neutral-100:#EFEBF7; --neutral-200:#E0DAF0;
  --neutral-300:#C7BEE0; --neutral-400:#A79CC4; --neutral-500:#867BA3;
  --neutral-600:#6A6086; --neutral-700:#514868; --neutral-800:#39324B;
  --neutral-900:#231E31; --neutral-950:#14101E;
}
/* light: bg neutral-50, text neutral-900, purple-700 headings, coral-700 as coral text */
/* dark:  bg neutral-950, text neutral-100, purple-400 + coral-400 accents */

Two colours, flat. Every pairing verified against WCAG AA in light and dark. Nothing is pure white or black.
