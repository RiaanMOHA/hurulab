## 3 · How We Solve It

### 我們先懂你的生意，再動手做。

我們先坐到你這一邊：了解你的營運怎麼跑、卡在哪裡，
再用真正的工程能力，把那個痛點拿掉。
不是拿著技術找地方用，而是從你的問題出發。

### We start with your business, not the technology.

First, we sit on your side of the table: how your operation actually runs, and where it hurts.
Then we apply real engineering to remove that pain.
Not a technology looking for a use case — your problem, solved.

## Copy rules applied

- Leads with what the client achieves; AI named only inside the client's pain, never as our identity — no buzzword positioning.
- Empathy first (pains 1–2), conversion second (CTA) — matches the website goal from today's meeting.
- No pricing anywhere; process is implied by "discovery" and resolved on the call.
- CTA framed as step zero: free, no commitment — and it filters both ways ("we'll say that too").
