# hurulab, the brand foundation

The single source of truth for what hurulab is. Everything else in this project sits on
this file. Built from evidence in `research/`, not from adjectives. Where proof is missing
it says so rather than filling the hole.

Written 30 July 2026. Name set lowercase, hurulab, by owner decision on 30 July. Note that
this overrides the 6 July brand guidelines, which say the mark is not set in all lowercase.

---

## 1. The scorecard

Four dimensions. Each is marked strong, thin or empty, with the proof found for it.

### Quality commitment: strong

The heaviest dimension of the four, and the best evidenced.

- **jadegia storefront, 14 May to 3 July 2026, seven weeks.** The rules were written before
  any screen was drawn: a behavioral floor defining how buttons, spacing, focus and motion
  must behave on every device, with the brand built on top of it. Two full directions were
  built in parallel on a shared base so the client chose by using them, not by guessing from
  a mockup. Jade at or above NT$200,000 shows "available by appointment" and cannot be added
  to the bag, so the commerce model cannot be misconfigured.
- **The value-add prototype.** 28 production step components. Apple's own interface guidance
  was overruled on three named points with the reason recorded each time: no frosted glass,
  REM and Noto Sans JP instead of San Francisco, and #F9F9F9 instead of true black. The
  working rule, in writing: "do not round rgba(255,255,255,0.72) to 0.7. The prototype is the
  spec." One locked copy source feeds both the iPad app and the PDF, enforced in code review,
  so the pitch and the leave-behind cannot drift apart.
- **The 3D house.** A real property rebuilt from a blueprint at 400 dpi and 933 interior
  photos, one room at a time, every measurement traceable to a named source. 76 commits
  across 8 merged pull requests, 22 April to 22 May 2026.
- **The map.** 121 commits across 25 pull requests over about 20 days, one feature per pull
  request.
- **The record itself.** 74 handoff journals carrying context between sessions. 360 content
  strings with all 8 conflicts decided by a human. Five AI audits caught guessing and sent
  back until every claim was observed rather than assumed.

### Sincerity: strong

Proved by what was thrown away, which is the kind that counts.

- A finished page-transition effect, built across several rounds, was deleted because jadegia
  read cleaner without it.
- Frosted glass was killed across all 20 steps of the pitch and the depth rebuilt from a 1px
  border and a shadow.
- About a third of a page was cut. The splash screen was killed once it stopped earning its
  place. A 14px minimum text size was held.
- The deck format itself was refused. Rather than making better slides, the team diagnosed
  how investors actually decide and built a different instrument.
- Riaan declined to split a credit and framed the work as a team outcome.
- Rules on record from the interviews: never take a client we cannot truly help (Henry). We
  never take the order, we solve the business problem, or we say no (Cal). Never ship slop,
  and never take a company that will not accept change (Riaan). Never sacrifice quality, and
  never burn out the team (Pedro).
- The case studies publish their own gaps. "No external investor has seen the finished deck."
  Written into the evidence file rather than left out of it.

### Heritage: thin, and now grounded

There is no founding date for hurulab anywhere in the 94 files. What exists instead is a
catalyst that is specific, dated and checkable.

- **MoreHarvest is a property firm with more than 20 years of trading behind it,** working a
  Kumamoto opportunity and raising from Singapore family offices and Taiwan capital.
- **The catalyst: a pitch deck died in the room.** The audience had seen a hundred like it,
  and a hundred and one did not move them.
- **What happened next is the origin.** MoreHarvest did not hire a design agency and a
  development shop, the path that costs months and six figures. The team built the thing
  itself, directing AI, and the CEO walked into the next room holding an instrument instead
  of a document.
- **Then outsiders asked for the same thing.** jadegia came because the alternatives were
  oversized with long delivery tails. Inkslap came because their previous partners were led
  by weak process that produced lacklustre delivery.
- **The grievance line is real but undated.** "We have been a frustrated client and we refuse
  to repeat vendors' flaws", ratified by Henry on 9 July. No vendor is named, no incident is
  recorded, and no date is attached. It was dropped from the 29 July positioning page without
  Henry being told.

### Symbolism: empty

- "We make ourselves useless", meaning the client keeps the product, the tools and the
  training and only needs hurulab once, was confirmed by the owner on 30 July as a marketing
  idea rather than something observed.
- No client has run alone after a handover. There is no instance behind it.
- It does not go on any page as a claim until there is one. It may be used as an intention,
  stated as an intention.

## 4. The brand

In trust order: where it came from, what it stands for, what it can prove, what it means.

### Origin

A property firm with twenty years behind it walked into a room in Singapore with a pitch deck
and watched it die. The audience had seen a hundred like it. A hundred and one did not move
them.

The obvious next move was to hire a design agency and a development shop, wait months, and
spend six figures. MoreHarvest did not do that. The team built the thing itself, directing
AI, and the CEO went into the next room holding something he could drive with his hands
rather than something he read aloud.

Then other companies started asking for the same thing. A jade house whose pieces sit in
worth beside Cartier, tired of alternatives that were oversized with long delivery tails. A
company that had already been let down by partners running weak process.

hurulab is the team from those rooms, now doing it for other people.

### Principle

The work is judged by what gets removed from it.

A page-transition effect took several rounds to build and was deleted, because the jade house
read cleaner without it. Frosted glass was killed across twenty steps of the pitch and the
depth rebuilt out of a one pixel border and a shadow. A third of a page went. A splash screen
went once it stopped earning its place. When Apple's own guidance was wrong for the work, it
was overruled on three specific points and the reason was written down each time.

The rules the team set for itself say the same thing from the other side. Never take a client
we cannot truly help. Never take the order, solve the business problem, or say no. Never ship
slop. Never take a company that will not accept change. Never burn out the team.

### Craft and results

The rules get written before the pixels. On the jade house, that meant a behavioral floor
defining how every button, space, focus state and motion behaves on every device, agreed
before a single screen was drawn, so quality could not quietly erode under a deadline. Seven
weeks, 14 May to 3 July 2026.

The client chooses by using, not by looking. Two complete directions were built in parallel
on a shared base so the choice could be made against real, working software rather than
against a picture of it.

The decisions that usually get left to engineering are made by design, on purpose. Jade at or
above NT$200,000 shows "available by appointment" and cannot be added to the bag. The
commerce model cannot be set up wrong, because the rule is in the system rather than in
somebody's head.

The pace is real and it is on the record. 76 commits across 8 merged pull requests between 22
April and 22 May for a house rebuilt in 3D from a blueprint at 400 dpi and 933 photographs.
121 commits across 25 pull requests in about 20 days for the map. 28 production components
for the pitch. 74 handoff journals so nothing has to be remembered twice.

And the work is checked. Five AI audits came back wrong and were sent back five times, until
every claim was observed instead of guessed. When a blueprint said a window sill sat at 2
meters and the photographs showed 0.85, the photographs won.

### Meaning

Not established. The intended meaning is independence: the client keeps the product, the
tools and the knowledge, and does not need hurulab again. That is currently an intention with
nothing behind it, and it is recorded here as an intention so that nobody publishes it as a
result.

## 5. What this foundation forbids

Direct consequences of the evidence above. These are constraints, not preferences.

- **Do not claim a measured outcome.** There is no analytics on any client site and no
  completed engagement on the current model. Naming a measure is allowed. Claiming a result
  is not.
- **Do not publish an invented case study.** The 29 July positioning page carried three, and
  they were written to be plausible rather than drawn from real work. Real ones exist. Use
  those.
- **Do not show a placeholder client logo.** A row reading "client one, client two"
  advertises that the answer is empty. Say the names are not yet ours to publish, or say
  nothing.
- **Do not claim a cold win.** There has not been one.
- **Do not lead with "we make ourselves useless".** No instance exists.
